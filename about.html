About Mr. Cheesecake

The story behind the name.

Hi, I'm Todd.

Hi, I'm Todd Robinson, and I love baking.

The first thing I ever baked was sugar cookies from a Sesame Street activity book I got from my late Aunt Renee when I was in elementary school.

I was so bored one day, I cracked open the book, did some puzzles, colored some Sesame Street characters, then found the recipe for sugar cookies. I thought it was pretty cool, I had fun, and I ended up with some confections in the process.

But I fell in love with baking when I made my first cheesecake for a family Thanksgiving dinner. I was told by my grandmother, Ms. Dorothy, that I had to bring a dish, but when I asked what I could bring, she admitted that she thought pretty much everything was covered.

Me being the lover of sweets that I am, I inquired what was for dessert. She mentioned a few aunts were bringing pies and cakes…

Then it hit me. I told her I would bring cheesecake.

Being the first family function I had gone to in a while, I really wanted to impress them — impress myself, really. I looked through a bunch of recipes I found online, until I found one I thought would be really amazing.

I really put my heart into the whole process, using the best ingredients, really taking my time with the recipe, and just thinking how much everyone would love my dessert.

And love it they did. Everyone at Thanksgiving dinner raved about how delicious the cheesecake was, and how surprised they all were that I was such a good baker.

And year after year I would make cheesecakes and baked goods for family functions, celebrations, birthdays, and celebrations of life. For family and friends alike.

And now I would like to share my love for cheesecakes and other baked goodies with my local community, surrounding area, and you.

Thanks for stopping by my new website. It's been years getting to a point where I feel ready to bring my cheesecakes to the community at large. I hope you'll give me a chance to be your Mr. Cheesecake. I hope you'll find something you'd like me to make for you from my selection of loafs, brownies, cakes, and cheesecakes!

Enjoy!
